Court lets state AI-bias claims against Workday proceed — even for out-of-state applicants

On June 22, a federal judge (N.D. Cal.) refused to dismiss state discrimination claims in Mobley v. Workday, ruling that California's FEHA can reach non-California plaintiffs because Workday is headquartered there and its AI screening tools are built and maintained there ("sufficient nexus to California"). The judge let an ADA claim proceed — alleging rejections tied to screening on medical-leave and recovery patterns — while dismissing a race-based disparate-impact claim and the theory that Workday is itself liable as an "employer." It follows a June 2 ruling that shielded Workday's curated bias-testing data under privilege but ordered production of its EEO-1 and OFCCP filings. ↗ HR Dive ↗ Duane Morris
