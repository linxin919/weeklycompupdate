01
Agentic AI shifted from "coming soon" to "shipping this week"

SmartRecruiters' autonomous CRM, iCIMS Coalesce AI, SAP's suite-wide agents, Oracle's Fusion Agentic Apps, and Phenom's Included AI acquisition all converge on same message: agents execute work, not assist humans. 02
Integration velocity as competitive signal: acquisition-to-product shipping in 4–6 months
Workday shipped Sana (acquired Sept 2025) in 4 months (March 2026). SAP integrated SmartRecruiters in ~6 months (deal close Jan 2026, product launch March/April). Phenom acquired Included AI (April 17) and Be Applied (Feb 10) with rapid integration roadmaps. This compression of post-deal integration timelines is becoming a visible competitive pressure — the ability to move fast post-acquisition is now a moat. ↗ Workday
03
HCM + TA convergence is now structural (not optional)
Three separate data points this week: SAP embedded SmartRecruiters inside SuccessFactors (single login, data sync), Oracle positioned HCM agents as buyers' "first stop" for TA workflows, Workday's Sana spans both HR and TA processes. The pattern is no longer "HCM vendors adding TA" — it's "TA is now native to HCM platforms." Standalone TA vendors face structural headwind unless they're category leaders (iCIMS, Phenom) or specialized (Eightfold, Beamery). ↗ SAP
04
Beamery and Eightfold are visibly quiet this week
No April announcements, launches, partnerships, or events found for either company. Both are mature unicorns ($1B+ valuations) with established products (Task Intelligence, AI Interviewer) still in market, but this week's agentic wave saw zero amplification or positioning update from either. Observable gap worth tracking: are they holding for a future announcement, or ceding momentum to larger platforms? ↗ Eightfold press ↗ Beamery news
